## Aesthetic & Design Rules

Inspired by Teenage Engineering, Teenage Mechanical Keyboards (MK-78), and Apple Pro Hardware:
- **Palette**: Clean Alabaster White (`#F6F6F4`), Pitch Matte Black (`#111111`), High-Voltage Safety Orange (`#FF4F00`).
- **Typography**: Massive lowercase Neo-Grotesk headlines (`every question. / every answer.`) with ultra-tight tracking, paired with technical monospace metadata badges.
- **Physicality**: Floating tactile mechanical keycaps with realistic bevels and soft shadows.
- **Interaction**: Crisp black pill buttons with orange hover states, smooth 3D flip card console.
