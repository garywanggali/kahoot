## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in Warm Scandinavian Minimalism, blending the clarity of modernist layouts with the organic warmth of natural materials. The target audience includes creative professionals, boutique studios, and editorial platforms that value quiet luxury and deliberate composition.

The aesthetic prioritizes intentional whitespace and an editorial "grid-first" approach. By combining tactile textures—evoked through color and subtle elevation—with sharp, geometric precision, the UI creates an emotional response of calm authority. It avoids the clinical coldness of tech-centric minimalism in favor of a "lived-in" sophisticated atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette is inspired by natural fibers and wood. 

- **Primary (#593E2E):** Deep Espresso Walnut. Used for headers, primary text, and high-impact surfaces. It provides the "ink" that defines the structural hierarchy.
- **Secondary (#BF9B7A):** Warm Camel. Used for interactive elements, secondary actions, and subtle dividers.
- **Background (#FFF8F3 / #F2E6D8):** Warm Cream Linen. The base layer for all views, providing a soft, non-reflective canvas.
- **4 Choices:**
  - Option A: Olive Moss (#555934)
  - Option B: Warm Camel Sand (#BF9B7A)
  - Option C: Terracotta Ochre (#8C5B3E)
  - Option D: Deep Espresso Walnut (#593E2E)

Avoid pure black (#000) or pure white (#FFF). All surfaces should feel dyed or stained rather than bleached.

## Typography

- **Headlines:** Uses **EB Garamond**. It is set with tight tracking to emphasize its literary, high-authority character. Large headlines are the centerpiece of the layout.
- **Body & Data:** Uses **Hanken Grotesk**. This contemporary sans-serif ensures legibility for functional data and long-form descriptions.
- **Labels:** Use uppercase Hanken Grotesk with slight letter-spacing for navigation and metadata to distinguish from body content.
